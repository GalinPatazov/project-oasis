What do the temperature, humidity and air pressure tell us?

Temperature, humidity, and air pressure shape air quality
by controlling how fast pollutants form chemically,
how easily they disperse or get trapped near the ground,
and how moisture interacts with particles.

What are PM2.5 and PM10?

PM2.5 and PM10 are types of microscopic particulate matter (air pollution) made of tiny solid particles and liquid droplets suspended in the air.

How can people mitigate their exposure to PM2.5 and PM10 indoors?

- Place a portable air purifier with a HEPA filter
- Improve ventilation during cooking
- Avoid burning and smoke sources
- Keep windows shut during high-pollution outdoor events
